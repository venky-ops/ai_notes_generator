Machine learning basics:
- Supervised learning involves labeled data.
- Unsupervised learning tries to find structure.
- Regression predicts continuous values; classification predicts categories.

Neural nets:
Neural networks are inspired by brain neurons. They have layers. Backpropagation is used for training.
Activation functions like ReLU, sigmoid are used.
Common optimizers are SGD, Adam.

Practical tips:
Always split dataset into train/val/test.
Normalize or standardize inputs.
Check for overfitting and use regularization.
